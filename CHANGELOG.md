## [0.1.0] - 2026-04-17

### Added

- `invoice-extract-id` — skill for extracting structured JSON from Indonesian
  invoices, receipts, and tax invoices (faktur pajak). Includes worked examples
  for standard faktur pajak and handwritten UMKM kwitansi.
- `wa-reply-id` — skill for drafting Bahasa Indonesia WhatsApp customer service
  replies across three registers (Formal-Friendly, Casual-Warm, Strictly-Formal).
  Includes 10 scenario examples covering price inquiries, complaints, refunds,
  promotions, and B2B.
- Bilingual EN + ID README.
- MIT license, CI workflow (frontmatter lint on PR/push).
